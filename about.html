Our Story

Apex Learning Academy began with a simple belief: quality technology education should be within reach of every Pakistani student. In a country where demand for Coding and Artificial Intelligence skills is rising rapidly, quality education remains expensive and out of reach for most families.

Recognizing this gap, Mukesh Kewal founded Apex Learning Academy — a platform built on four principles: affordable fees, small batches with personal attention, practical project-based learning, and instruction in both Urdu and English.

Today, Apex Learning Academy is committed to becoming Pakistan's most trusted and affordable online tech academy — a place where every student can learn, grow, and achieve their dreams.

Our Mission

To deliver quality Coding and AI education to every Pakistani student — regardless of city, background, or financial capacity.

Our Vision

To become Pakistan's most trusted and affordable online tech academy — reaching 500+ students by 2028.
