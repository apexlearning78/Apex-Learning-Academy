About Apex Learning Academy

Quality tech education for every Pakistani student — affordable, practical, and personal.

Our Story

Apex Learning Academy began with a simple belief: quality technology education should be within reach of every Pakistani student. In a country where demand for Coding and Artificial Intelligence skills is rising rapidly, quality education remains expensive and out of reach for most families.

Recognizing this gap, Mukesh Kewal founded Apex Learning Academy — a platform built on four principles: affordable fees, small batches with personal attention, practical project-based learning, and instruction in both Urdu and English.

Our Mission

To deliver quality Coding and AI education to every Pakistani student — regardless of city, background, or financial capacity.

Our Vision

Year 1: Enroll 100+ students, add Data Science and Web Development courses, and build a strong student community across Pakistan.

Year 2: Reach 500+ students, launch advanced courses in Machine Learning and Deep Learning, hire expert instructors, and expand into smaller cities through online learning.

Our Teaching Philosophy

We believe that true learning happens through practice, not memorization. Every concept taught at Apex Learning Academy is followed by a real project. Every student receives individual attention. Every session is designed to be understood, not just heard.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions from students and parents.

What is the fee structure?

Coding course fee is Rs. 3,000 per month, and Artificial Intelligence course fee is Rs. 4,000 per month. Fees are payable in advance at the beginning of each month.

What are the class timings?

Classes are held in the evening from 6:00 PM to 8:00 PM, Monday through Saturday. A weekend special batch is also available for working professionals.

Are classes online or on-campus?

All classes are conducted 100% online through Google Meet or Jitsi Meet. Students can attend from anywhere in Pakistan.

Will I receive a certificate?

Yes. Upon successful completion of the course and final project submission, students receive a professional certificate from Apex Learning Academy.

Can complete beginners join?

Absolutely. Our courses start from zero. No prior coding or technical knowledge is required. We build your skills from the ground up.

Is the demo class really free?

Yes. Your first demo class is completely free. You can experience our teaching style and course structure before making any payment.

How can I pay the fees?

We accept payments through JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and direct Bank Transfer. Payment details are shared after registration confirmation.

What is the batch size?

We maintain small batches of 10 to 15 students to ensure every student receives personal attention and individual doubt clearing.

What if I miss a class?

Recorded sessions are provided for a limited time so you can catch up on missed content. Live doubt clearing sessions are also available.

Do I need a laptop?

A laptop is strongly recommended for coding practice. However, students can begin with a desktop computer as well. A stable internet connection is essential.
