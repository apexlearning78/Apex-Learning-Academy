Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining Apex Learning Academy.

What is the fee structure?
Coding course fee is Rs. 3,000 per month, and Artificial Intelligence course fee is Rs. 4,000 per month. Fees are payable in advance at the beginning of each month.
What are the class timings?
Classes are held in the evening from 6:00 PM to 8:00 PM, Monday through Saturday. A weekend special batch is also available for working professionals.
Are classes online or on-campus?
All classes are conducted 100% online through Google Meet or Jitsi Meet. Students can attend from anywhere in Pakistan.
Will I receive a certificate?
Yes. Upon successful completion of the course and final project submission, students receive a professional certificate from Apex Learning Academy with a unique verification ID.
Can complete beginners join?
Absolutely. Our courses start from zero. No prior coding or technical knowledge is required. We build your skills from the ground up.
Is the demo class really free?
Yes. Your first demo class is completely free. You can experience our teaching style and course structure before making any payment.
